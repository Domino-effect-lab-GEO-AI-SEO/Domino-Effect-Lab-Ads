# About Us — Domino Effect Lab Ads



# Domino Effect Lab Ads

**Generative Engine Optimisation and AI visibility for businesses that want to be found, cited, and recommended in AI-generated answers.**

Domino Effect Lab Ads is an Ireland-based AI visibility and Generative Engine Optimisation agency. We help businesses understand how AI systems currently see them, where they are missing, and what to change so they can appear more accurately and more often in AI-generated answers.

As search shifts from clicks to answers, traditional visibility is no longer enough on its own. Customers now ask AI assistants for recommendations, comparisons, and summaries before they ever visit a website. Our work helps businesses adapt to that shift with a practical mix of diagnostics, technical fixes, content structure, and ongoing improvement.

---

## What we do

We help businesses improve how they are understood and represented across AI-powered search and answer systems.

Our work includes:

- **AI visibility diagnostics**  
  We assess whether AI assistants can find your business, how accurately they describe it, and where competitors are being recommended instead.

- **Technical and structural fixes**  
  We improve the parts of your web presence that affect AI understanding, including entity consistency, schema, answer blocks, and source clarity.

- **Content and authority optimisation**  
  We strengthen the content, signals, and supporting assets that help AI systems retrieve, trust, and cite your business.

- **Measurement and improvement**  
  We baseline what answer engines say about your brand today, then track change over time using practical visibility metrics.

## Products and reports

### Intelligence and diagnostic products

- **AI Visibility Footprint Scanner**  
  Maps where your brand appears across web sources and shows which AI models can see your business and where the gaps are.

- **The AI Pulse Visibility Scorecard**  
  Measures how accurately and prominently AI assistants represent your brand.

- **Hallucination Risk & Brand Safety Audit**  
  Detects when AI assistants fabricate or distort information about your business.

- **Rival Radar: AI Competitive Visibility Report**  
  Compares your AI visibility against competitors.

- **AI Source Index Research Report**  
  Maps how AI models weigh different content types.

### Content and authority products

- **FAQ Schema**  
  Generates grounded FAQ content with JSON-LD schema.

- **txt.llm**  
  Creates a structured brand-truth file for AI systems.

- **Brand Message Multiplier**  
  Optimises core messaging across multiple channels.
